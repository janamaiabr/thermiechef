About ThermieChef

Hi, I'm Aly 👋

I'm Brazilian, I live in Australia, and I'm a little obsessed with one thing: making seriously good food ridiculously easy. My Thermomix is my sous-chef — it weighs, blends, steams and cooks — so I get the joy of cooking and skip the fuss and the mountain of pans.

My favourite thing? Taking the dishes everyone dreams about — from the world's best chefs to the Brazilian classics I grew up on — and reimagining them for the Thermomix, so you can pull them off on a busy Tuesday and watch the whole table go quiet for a second.

Come cook with me — and whenever you're ready for a Thermomix of your own, I'm right here to help you choose. 🧡

What ThermieChef is all about

ThermieChef is an independent recipe site that takes iconic dishes from the world's most celebrated chefs — Julia Child, Yotam Ottolenghi, Jamie Oliver, Nigella Lawson, Stephanie Alexander and many more — and reimagines them for the Thermomix TM6 and TM7. Every recipe includes exact speeds, temperatures and step-by-step instructions so you can reproduce restaurant-quality dishes at home.

This is a homage, not an endorsement. Each recipe is Chef Aly's independent reinterpretation, crafted with respect for the original chef's legacy but adapted for the Thermomix platform and Australian home cooks.
